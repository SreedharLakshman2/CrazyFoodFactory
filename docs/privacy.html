Privacy Policy

Kido Chef by Sai Laksha Technologies

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Kids first

Kido Chef is made for children. The game is designed to be used without an account and without sharing personal information.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information, names, emails, contacts, photos, location, or advertising identifiers. There is no login and no analytics SDK.

Stars, unlocked rewards, and sound settings are saved only on the device (using on-device storage). You can erase that progress from Settings.

Internet

Gameplay works completely offline. Sharing a reward card uses the system share sheet on your device. We do not run our own servers for play data.

Photos

If a grown-up chooses Share Reward, iOS may ask for permission to save the card. That image stays under the family’s control.
