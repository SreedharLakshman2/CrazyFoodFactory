Kido Chef by Sai Laksha Technologies

Need a hand in the kitchen?

Kido Chef is a kids cooking game. It works fully offline. There is no login, no ads, and no tracking. Progress and sound settings stay on the device.

  • Turn music, sounds, and chef voice on or off in Settings.
  • Reset stars and levels from Settings if you want a fresh start.
  • Open Ingredient School from Home to learn what foods are and how we cook them.
